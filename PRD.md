# 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose of The Product Requirements Document(PRD):

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) outlines the requirements, objectives and scope of the proposed Babcock University Lost & Found System.

It explains the problem the system is intended to solve, the needs of its users and the features the system should provide. It also serves as a guide for the development team and relevant university personnel, ensuring that everyone involved understands what the system is expected to do.

The document will guide the development of a system that makes reporting, managing and recovering lost items easier within the Babcock University community.

### 1.2 Product Overview:

The BULFS System is a digital platform that makes it easier for students and the Babcock community to report, hold and recover lost items

Our platform will allow students to report missing items, while authorized uni personnel will be able to manage all items submitted to the BULFS office.

The system helps connect reports of missing items with records of recovered items, making it easier for uni personnel to identify the rightful owners and contact them.

University personnel will remain responsible for keeping recovered items safe, verifying ownership and releasing it to the respectful Owners. Students will collect their items from the BULFS office after providing the required identification and proof of ownership.

The system will support the university's existing Lost \& Found process rather than by communicating effectively,holding items and recovery of lost items more organized

### 1.3 Background and Context:

At BU, students who lose personal items often rely on WhatsApp groups, or they ask the people around the area where the item was last seen. These methods are good about spreading information on missing items, but they do not provide a central platform where reports can be submitted and duely followed up.

When recovered items are handed over to uni personnel, they are recorded and kept at the BULFS office. However, there is no established method for connecting the records of missing items to the found items at the BULFS office, making it difficult to identify and contact the rightful owners.

The university previously had a Lost & Found system, but it was discontinued several years ago. The specific reasons for its discontinuation are unclear. Since then, the process has relied on posting on whatsapp departmental groups and the existing physical procedures handled by university personnel.

These challenges showed us the need for a more organized approach that connects students with missing items to the personnel managing recovered items. The BULFS System wants to provide this connection through a centralized platform while maintaining the university existing physical collection Process.

### 1.4 Problem Statement:

Students mostly rely on WhatsApp groups, or asking people around to report or search for lost items. However, these methods are not well organized, and most students receive little or no response amd follow-up after reporting their missing items.

Uni personnel also face difficulties identifying the owners of recovered items. Although they record and keep these items at the BULFS office, there is no centralized way to match them with student's report

Our BULFS System tends to solve this problem by providing a central platform where students can report missing items and uni personnel can record recovered items, identify possible owners and contact them.

# 2. Product Vision and Objectives

### 2.1 Product Vision

To create a centralized platform where students can report lost items and uni personnel can easily identify owners and return found items.

### 2.2 Product Goals:

1. Provide students with a central platform to report missing items.
2. Allow uni personnel to record and hold found items.
3. Make it easier to match reports of missing items with records of found items.
4. Notify the relevant university personnel whenever a student submits a report.
5. Improve communication and follow-up between students and uni personnel.
6. Ensure that ownership is verified through official means and recovered items are released.

### 2.3 Expected Outcomes:

The BULFS System is expected to bring the following improvements:

1. Students will have a simpler way to report lost items.
2. Uni personnel will be able to keep track of found items more easily.
3. It will be easier to connect reports of missing items with items that have been found.
4. Students will receive follow-ups and updates on their reports.
5. More lost belongings will have a better chance of reaching their rightful owners.

# 3. User Research and Analysis

### 3.1 Users Stories

We conducted face-to-face interviews with 10 participants: 7 students and 3 university personnel.
The students were asked about their experiences with losing belongings,

The university personnel were asked how they receive, record, store and return items that have been found.

The responses helped us understand how the current process works, identify the challenges faced by both groups and determine areas that need improvement.

### 3.2 Target Users

The Babcock University Lost & Found System is intended for two main groups of users:

1. Students: Students will use the platform to report lost items, providing as much details as possible about missing items and receive updates and follow-ups about their reports.

2. University Personnel: University personnel will use the platform to record found items, manage those items , review possible matches with lost-item reports and contact potential owners. They will also remain responsible for verifying ownership and releasing recovered items to their rightful owners.

### 3.3 Research Findings

The interviews with students and university personnel revealed the following:

1. Students rely on posting on departmental whatsapp groups and asking people around that area where the item was last seen.
2. Most students receive little or no response and follow-up after reporting their lost items.
3. University personnel record details of found items. However, they do not have an established way to identify and contact their owners.
4. Found items are kept at the BULFS office. Before collecting an item, a person must provide identification and proof of ownership.

# 4. Product Scope

### 4.1 In-Scope Features

The first version of the BULFS System will include the following features:

1. It would allow users to create accounts and log in to the system.
2. It would also allow users to report lost items by providing as much details as possible to identify the items.
3. It would allow Uni Personnel to record new recovered items
4. It allow uni personnel to view, update and manage lost and found item records.
5. Notify the relevant university personnel whenever a new lost-item report is submitted.
6. Help university personnel compare lost-item reports with records of found items to identify possible matches.
7. Allow users to receive updates about their reports.
8. Ensure that university personnel confirm a person's identity and proof of ownership before releasing a recovered item.

### 4.3 MVP Definition

The first version of the BULFS System will focus on the basic features needed to report and recover lost items.

Users will be able to create accounts, log in and report lost items, while uni personnel would be able to record found items, manage reports, receive notifications and identify possible matches

Users will also receive updates about their reports, while university personnel will remain responsible for verifying ownership and releasing recovered items.

The system can be improved with additional features in the future.

# 5. Functional Requirements.

### 5.1 User Registration and Login:

The system shall:

1. Allow users to create accounts using their UMIS details.
2. Allow registered users to log in securely
3. Allow users to log out of their accounts.
4. Restrict access to authorized university personnel features to approved personnel and prevent any unauthorized user from accessing the system.

### 5.2 Lost Item Reporting:

The system shall:

1. Allow users submit reports of lost items.
2. Allow users to provide about as much details as possible about describing the items ns upload images if available.
3. It saves Reports and notifies uni personnel and allows users check the status of their reports.

### 5.3 Found Item Registration:

The System shall:

1. Allow uni personnel register items that have been found.
2. Allow personnel to enter the details about the item.
3. Save the records and update when necessary.

### 5.4 Notifications:

The system shall:

1. Notify uni personnel whenever a new lost item report is submitted
2. Notify Users when there is an update on their reports.
3. Notify uni personnel when a user submit a claim for a found tem for collection

# 6. Non Functional Requirements

The System shall:

1. Be secure enough to protect users accounts through secure login, restrict access to unathorized users and personnel and store information.
2. Have a simple and clear layout for users to report lost items and track their status of reports and would be responsive on both laptop and phones.
3. Load Pages and display information within a reasonable timeframe,allow users search and view for items smoothly
4. Be accessible to users and uni personnel at all times and be available to both mobile phones and computers with internet condition.

# 7. Success Metrics

The success of the system will be measure by:
1 The speed of how users report lost items easily. 2. Amount of lost items returned to their owners. 3. Quick Response by time by Uni Personnel. 4. User satisfaction, if they find the system helpful and easy to use.

# 9. Constraints, Assumptions and Risks

The system will require an internet connection and a device to work and also depend on the uni persoonal record of found items and verified ownership.

Possible challenges include poor internet access, limited resources, delayed responses and false claims on recovered items.

These challenges can be dropped through proper system management, secure accounts and proper ownership checks

# 10. Conclusion

The proposed BULFS System aims to make reporting and recovering lost belongings easier and more organized.
By providing a central platform for reporting lost items, recording found items and improving communication, the system will help students and uni personnel manage lost and found belongings more effectively. Overall, the system is expected to improve the chances of reuniting lost items with their rightful owners.
